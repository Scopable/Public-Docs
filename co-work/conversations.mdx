Co-Work conversations are persistent chat sessions — you can start a new one at any time, return to an old one to pick up where you left off, or run multiple in parallel across different clients. Your full conversation history is saved automatically, so nothing is lost when you navigate away.

## Starting a New Conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Co-Work">
    Click the **Co-Work icon** in the left navigation bar to open the Co-Work panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new conversation">
    Click **New Conversation** at the top of the conversation list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your first message">
    Type your question or request and press **Enter** (or click **Send**). Co-Work uses the current client context and any active skills to respond.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Background Runs

If Co-Work is working on a long-running task — such as analyzing a large client environment or processing a multi-step action — you can navigate freely without interrupting it.

- The task continues in the background while you work on other clients or pages.
- A **status indicator** appears in the Co-Work panel and in the navigation bar showing that a run is in progress.
- When the task finishes, the indicator updates and you can return to the conversation to review the result.

<Tip>
  Start a background run for time-consuming analyses — like a full compliance review or multi-client assessment — and move on to other work. Co-Work will have the results ready when you come back.
</Tip>

## Finding Past Conversations

Use the **search bar** at the top of the conversation list to search by keyword across all your past conversations. Results match against conversation names and message content.

You can also scroll the list chronologically — conversations are ordered by most recent activity by default.

## Renaming a Conversation

1. Hover over a conversation in the list.
2. Click the **rename icon** (pencil) that appears.
3. Type a new name and press **Enter**.

<Tip>
  Give conversations descriptive names like *Acme IT — Q3 QBR prep* or *Riverside Dental — compliance gap analysis* so you can find them quickly later. Co-Work auto-generates a name from your first message, but a specific name is easier to scan.
</Tip>

## Archiving a Conversation

Archiving removes a conversation from your main list without deleting it. Use this to keep your list focused without permanently losing older work.

1. Hover over the conversation.
2. Click **Archive**.

To view archived conversations, open the **Views** dropdown in the conversation list and select **Archived**.

## Deleting a Conversation

1. Hover over the conversation.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Confirm deletion in the prompt.

<Warning>
  Deleting a conversation permanently removes it and all its message history. This action cannot be undone. Archive conversations you might want to reference later instead of deleting them.
</Warning>
