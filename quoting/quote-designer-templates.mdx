Templates define exactly how your quotes look when clients receive them. A well-built template reinforces your brand, makes pricing easy to read, and presents your scope professionally — in both the client-facing web view and the downloaded PDF. This guide walks you through creating a template from scratch, applying it to a specific quote, and setting it as your account default.

---

## Create a Template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Quote Designer">
    Go to **Settings** in the left sidebar and select **Quote Designer**. Click
    **New Template** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name your template">
    Give the template a descriptive name so you can identify it later — for
    example, "Standard Managed Services" or "Hardware Proposal – Dark Theme".
    The name is internal only; clients never see it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the cover block">
    The cover block is the first thing your client sees. Set it up to make a
    strong first impression:

    - **Logo** — upload your company logo in PNG or SVG format (max 2 MB). The logo appears on the cover and optionally in the header of subsequent pages.
    - **Brand color** — choose a hex color for accents, headings, and the cover background stripe.
    - **Tagline** — add an optional short phrase beneath your logo, such as your company slogan or a quote-specific message like "Prepared exclusively for [Client Name]".

    The cover block automatically pulls in the client name, quote number, and
    quote date from the quote record — you don't need to add those manually.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add flowing sections">
    Flowing sections make up the body of the quote. Add and reorder them by
    dragging each section block into the sequence that works best for your
    quote format:

    | Section type | What it renders |
    |---|---|
    | **Line items table** | A formatted table of all products with quantities, unit prices, and totals |
    | **Scope narrative** | Free-text or template-text blocks for describing the engagement, deliverables, or implementation details |
    | **Terms and conditions** | Your standard T&C copy, rendered as a readable text block before the closing section |

    You can add multiple scope narrative blocks to separate different phases or
    service categories within the same quote.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the closing block">
    The closing block appears at the end of the quote. Configure it to wrap up
    the document professionally:

    - **Signature area** — include a signature line (or leave it off for quotes that are accepted via the web link)
    - **Contact details** — add your name, email, and phone so the client knows who to reach out to
    - **Closing copy** — an optional short paragraph such as a call to action or next-step instructions
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview the PDF">
    Before saving, click **Preview PDF** to render the template with sample
    data. Check that your logo loads, brand color appears correctly, section
    order looks right, and the closing block lands on the final page. Make any
    adjustments and preview again until the output matches your expectations.

    <Tip>
      The PDF renders with fixed-width page dimensions. The web-link view uses a
      responsive layout that adapts to the client's screen size. Always preview
      both before using a template for the first time.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the template">
    Click **Save Template**. The template is now available to select on any
    quote in your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Select a Template for a Quote

You can apply any saved template to an individual quote, overriding the account default for that specific quote.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the quote">
    Navigate to the quote you want to update — either from the client record or
    from the **Quotes** list in the left sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Template picker">
    In the quote detail view, click the **Template** selector. A dropdown lists
    all templates saved in your account, with the account default pre-selected.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your template">
    Select the template you want to use. The quote preview in the right panel
    updates to show how the quote will look with that template applied.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview the client-facing view">
    Click **Preview** to open the client-facing quote view in a new tab. This
    is the same experience your client will see when they open the quote link.
    Confirm the layout, branding, and content all look correct before sending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set an Account Default Template

The account default template is automatically applied to every new quote. Setting a strong default means your team gets consistent branding without having to manually select a template for each quote.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Quote Designer settings">
    Go to **Settings → Quote Designer**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the template to set as default">
    Locate the template you want to use as the account-wide default in the
    template list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mark it as Default">
    Click the **⋯** menu on that template row and select **Set as Default**. A
    **Default** badge appears next to the template name. All new quotes created
    from this point forward will use this template unless a different one is
    manually selected on the quote.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Changing the account default does not update templates already selected on
  existing quotes. Only new quotes pick up the new default automatically.
</Note>
