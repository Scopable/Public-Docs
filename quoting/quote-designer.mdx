Quote Designer lets you create branded PDF and web-quote templates that reflect your company's identity. Upload your logo, set brand colors, build a cover page, arrange flowing content sections, and add a professional closing block — all without touching any code. Every template you create in Quote Designer controls exactly how your quotes look when clients receive them, whether they're viewing the public quote link in their browser or opening a downloaded PDF.

## How Quote Designer Templates Work

A template is a reusable layout that attaches to a quote. When you send a quote or download it as a PDF, Scopable renders the quote's data — line items, scope descriptions, pricing, and terms — through the template's structure and branding. Changing a template's design after a quote has been sent does not retroactively update the client's existing link; it applies to newly sent quotes and fresh PDF downloads.

Every template is built from three core blocks:

| Block | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Cover block** | The first page or header: your logo, brand color, tagline, client name, and quote date |
| **Flowing quote sections** | The body: line items table, scope narrative, and terms and conditions, arranged in the order you choose |
| **Closing block** | The final section: signature area, contact details, and any custom closing copy |

## Access Quote Designer

You can reach Quote Designer from two places:

- **Settings → Quote Designer** — manage all templates, create new ones, and set your account default
- **Inside the Quote Wizard** — select or preview a template during the delivery step of a quote build

<Note>
  You need the **Manage Tenant Settings** permission to create or edit Quote
  Designer templates. If you can view templates but can't save changes, ask your
  Scopable Admin to grant you this permission.
</Note>
