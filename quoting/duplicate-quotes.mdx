When you need to quote a similar scope for the same client — an annual renewal, a revised option, or a near-identical engagement — duplicating an existing quote saves you from rebuilding from scratch. The duplicate starts as a fresh draft with your prior line items and structure intact, so you only need to make the changes that matter.

## How to Duplicate a Quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the quote you want to duplicate">
    Navigate to the client record or go to **Quotes** in the left sidebar. Open
    the quote you want to use as your starting point. The quote can be in any
    status — draft, sent, approved, or closed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Actions menu">
    Click the **Actions** menu (the three-dot or kebab menu) in the top-right
    corner of the quote detail page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Duplicate">
    Click **Duplicate** from the Actions menu. Scopable immediately creates a
    new draft quote and opens it for editing. The original quote is untouched.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and adjust the duplicate">
    Make any changes to the new draft — update quantities, adjust pricing, or
    swap out products — before sending it through the approval and delivery flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Copies and What Resets

Understanding exactly what carries over helps you avoid surprises when you open the duplicate.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="What is copied">
    The following fields and settings transfer from the original to the
    duplicate:

    | Field | Notes |
    |---|---|
    | Products | All line items, including part numbers and product names |
    | Quantities | Per-line quantities as they were in the original |
    | Scope descriptions | Client-facing line-item text and section narratives |
    | Template selection | The Quote Designer template applied to the original |
    | Section structure | Headers, groupings, and ordering of line items |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="What is reset">
    The following fields are cleared or regenerated so the duplicate behaves
    like a brand-new quote:

    | Field | Reset to |
    |---|---|
    | Quote number | Next sequential number in your account |
    | Status | Draft |
    | Sent timestamp | Cleared |
    | Approved timestamp | Cleared |
    | Approval status | Not submitted |
    | Client delivery link | New unique URL generated on first send |
    | Created date | Current date and time |
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Common Use Cases

**Annual renewal with updated quantities**
Duplicate last year's quote, adjust seat counts and pricing for the new term, then send it through your normal approval flow.

**Alternative options for the same scope**
Duplicate a quote to create an Option A / Option B pair. Adjust products or terms in each, then present both to the client.

**Repeatable service packages**
If you frequently quote similar managed services bundles, keep a well-structured "master" quote and duplicate it each time a new opportunity comes in.

<Warning>
  Duplicating a quote does **not** re-fetch pricing from your connected
  distributor integrations. The costs and sell prices are copied as static
  values from the original. If distributor rates have changed since the original
  quote was built, open the duplicate and update pricing manually before
  submitting for approval or sending to the client.
</Warning>
