Contacts in Scopable represent the people at each of your client organizations. Every contact record stores the details you need for day-to-day communication — name, email, phone, title, and address — and stays in sync with your connected PSA so your data is always current.

## Finding a Contact

Use the search bar at the top of any client's **Contacts** tab to filter by name, email, or phone number in real time. For programmatic lookups, call the API endpoint `GET /v1/contacts/search` with a `q` query parameter to search across all contacts.

```http title="Search contacts via API"
GET https://api.scopable.io/v1/contacts/search?q=jane+doe
Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY
```

## Updating a Contact

1. Open the client record and go to the **Contacts** tab.
2. Click the contact row to open the detail panel.
3. Edit any of the following fields: name, email, phone, title, or address.
4. Click **Save** to apply your changes.

<Note>
  Changes you make to contact records in Scopable do not write back to your PSA. Updates sync from your PSA to Scopable, not the other way around.
</Note>

## Adding a Contact Photo

1. Open the contact detail panel.
2. Click the avatar placeholder in the top-left corner.
3. Select a **JPG** or **PNG** file from your device (recommended: at least 200 × 200 px).
4. Crop if prompted, then click **Save**.

The photo appears in the contact list, the contact detail panel, and wherever that contact is referenced across Scopable.

## Marking Favorites

Click the **star icon** next to any contact to pin them to the top of the Contacts table. Favorites persist across sessions and are visible only to you — they do not affect other users' views.

<Tip>
  Mark your primary point of contact at each client as a favorite so they're always one click away when you're preparing a quote or QBR.
</Tip>

## Saving Table Views

Configure the Contacts table with your preferred columns, filters, and sort order, then click **Save View** and give the view a name. Saved Views let you return to the exact configuration instantly. See the [Saved Views](/clients/saved-views) page for full details.

## Exporting Contacts

1. Open the **Contacts** tab on any client record.
2. Apply any filters you want reflected in the export.
3. Click **Export → CSV**.

The download includes all visible columns for the filtered contact set.

## Archiving vs. Deleting

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Archive** | Hides the contact from active views; recoverable from the archived filter |
| **Delete** | Permanently removes a manually created contact |

<Warning>
  Contacts synced from your PSA are never permanently deleted from Scopable. If a contact is removed in your PSA, it will be marked inactive during the next sync rather than deleted.
</Warning>

To archive a contact, open the contact detail panel → click the **⋯ menu** → **Archive**.

## Default Contact

The contact marked as **Default** on a client record appears on the client card and is pre-populated as the delivery recipient when you create a quote. To change the default, open the contact detail panel → toggle **Set as Default**.

<Tip>
  Keep your Default contact up to date — it's the name and email that show up first when your client receives a quote or assessment.
</Tip>
