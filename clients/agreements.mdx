The Agreements table in Scopable pulls in contract data from your connected PSA and gives you a read-only view of active recurring revenue alongside the rest of your client record. You can filter, sort, inspect line items, and annotate agreements with internal comments — all without ever touching the source data in your PSA.

<Note>
  Scopable never modifies agreements in your PSA. The Agreements table is read-only. Any changes to agreement terms, line items, or status must be made directly in ConnectWise Manage or Halo PSA.
</Note>

## What You Can Do

The Agreements table supports the following actions:

- **Filter by status** — narrow the list to Active, Expired, or all agreements
- **Sort by value or renewal date** — click any column header to sort ascending or descending
- **Inspect line items** — click any agreement row to expand the detail panel and see individual line items, pricing, and dates
- **Add internal comments** — annotate agreements with notes that stay inside Scopable

## Filtering for Active Recurring Revenue

To see only your current MRR-generating agreements:

1. Open the client record and go to the **Agreements** tab.
2. Click the **Status** filter.
3. Select **Active**.

The table now shows only in-force agreements. The total contract value displayed updates to reflect the filtered set.

<Tip>
  Save this filtered view as your default so every time you open the Agreements tab it shows only active agreements sorted by renewal date. See [Saved Views](/clients/saved-views) for instructions.
</Tip>

## Inspecting Agreement Details

1. Click any row in the Agreements table to open the detail panel.
2. Review the **Summary** tab for contract dates, total value, and billing frequency.
3. Switch to the **Line Items** tab to see individual services, quantities, and unit prices.

## Adding Internal Comments

Comments let you and your team leave notes about an agreement — renewal risk, pricing history, or client conversation context — without affecting anything in your PSA.

1. Click the agreement row to open the detail panel.
2. Go to the **Comments** tab.
3. Type your note and press **Post Comment**.

Comments are visible to all users on your Scopable account and are timestamped with the author's name. They never write back to your PSA.

## Sorting by Renewal Date

Click the **Renewal Date** column header to sort agreements with the soonest renewals at the top. This is useful when preparing for QBRs or planning outreach ahead of contract renewals.

To maintain this view across sessions, save it using **Save View**. See [Saved Views](/clients/saved-views).
