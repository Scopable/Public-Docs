Setting up your Scopable account takes just a few minutes. This guide walks you through signing up for a free trial, configuring your workspace, inviting a teammate, and signing in — so your team can start building quotes and managing clients right away.

## Create your account

<Note>
  The free trial gives you full access to every Scopable feature — CPQ quoting, Co-Work AI, assessments, GRC compliance, and all integrations. No credit card is required to start.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up at scopable.io">
    Go to [scopable.io/signup](https://scopable.io/signup) and enter your name, work email address, and a password. Click **Create account** to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Scopable. Click the **Verify email** link inside. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or click **Resend verification email** on the confirmation screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace">
    After verification, Scopable prompts you to name your workspace. Use your company name or MSP brand name — this appears in your portal URL and in client-facing documents such as quotes and proposals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your first team member">
    On the final setup screen, enter a colleague's email address to send them an invitation. You can skip this step and invite more users later from **Settings → Users**. See [Manage Users and Permissions](/getting-started/users-and-permissions) for full details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in to Scopable

You can sign in to Scopable with your email and password, or with a passkey for a faster, passwordless experience.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email and password">
    1. Go to [app.scopable.io](https://app.scopable.io).
    2. Enter your email address and password.
    3. Click **Sign in**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Passkey">
    1. Go to [app.scopable.io](https://app.scopable.io).
    2. Click **Sign in with a passkey**.
    3. Follow your browser or device prompt to authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a hardware security key.

    You must add a passkey to your account before using this option. See [Sign In with Passkeys](/getting-started/passkeys) to get set up.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Bookmark [app.scopable.io](https://app.scopable.io) in your browser for instant access to your workspace without navigating through the marketing site.
</Tip>
