Scopable is a unified platform built for Managed Service Providers. It connects your PSA, RMM, Microsoft environment, and distributor catalogs into a single workspace so you can quote faster, run better QBRs, manage compliance, and automate repetitive work — all from live client data instead of stale spreadsheets.

## Get Started in Three Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at [scopable.io/signup](https://scopable.io/signup) — no credit card required. Your free trial gives you full access to the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your PSA or RMM">
    Head to **Integrations** in the left navigation and connect ConnectWise Manage, Halo PSA, NinjaOne, or another supported platform. Scopable syncs your clients, contacts, and configurations automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build Your First Quote">
    Open a client, click **New Quote**, and follow the Quote Wizard. Scopable pulls live pricing from your connected distributors so you're quoting from current data — not last month's spreadsheet.
  </Step>
</Steps>
