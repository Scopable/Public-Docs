Scopable starts with a free trial — no credit card required. When you're ready to subscribe, you'll choose a billing commitment, review your seat count, and complete checkout through Stripe. The whole process takes less than five minutes.

## Prerequisites

- You must have **Admin** permissions on your Scopable account to manage billing.
- Have your payment card details ready for Stripe Checkout.

## Subscribing to Scopable

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing settings">
    Go to **Settings → Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the subscription flow">
    Click **Upgrade** or **Start Subscription**. This opens the subscription configuration screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a commitment">
    Select **Monthly** or **Annual**.

    - **Monthly**: billed each month, cancel any time.
    - **Annual**: billed upfront for the full year at a discounted rate. Best if you're confident in your team size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your seat count">
    Scopable counts every active user on your account as a seat. Review the number shown — this is what you'll be billed for. If the count is wrong, go to **Settings → Users** to deactivate unused accounts before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Stripe Checkout">
    Click **Continue to Checkout**. You'll be redirected to Stripe's secure payment page. Enter your card details and billing address, then click **Subscribe**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your subscription">
    Stripe redirects you back to Scopable with your subscription active. You'll see a confirmation banner and your plan status in **Settings → Billing** will update to reflect your new subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Annual commitments are billed upfront for the full year. If you add seats after subscribing, those seats are prorated for the remainder of the annual period.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Invite your entire team and set all accounts to active before subscribing — the seat count at checkout sets your initial billing. It's easier to confirm the right count now than to adjust it after checkout.
</Tip>

## Managing Your Plan

After subscribing, manage all billing details from **Settings → Billing**:

| Action | Where |
|---|---|
| View your current plan and renewal date | Settings → Billing → Plan |
| Update your payment method | Settings → Billing → Payment Method |
| Download past invoices | Settings → Billing → Invoices |
| Upgrade or change commitment | Settings → Billing → Change Plan |
| Cancel your subscription | Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription |

<Warning>
  Cancelling your subscription does not immediately end your access. Your account remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, your account enters a read-only state and you will not be charged again.
</Warning>
