Scopable integrations pull your existing data into the platform so you can quote, run QBRs, and manage compliance from live information — not manual exports. Once an integration is connected, Scopable syncs on a regular schedule and surfaces that data across client records, quotes, assessments, and Co-Work AI — no copy-pasting required.

<Note>
  Connecting or managing integrations requires the **Manage Tenant Settings** permission. If you don't see the Integrations section in Settings, ask your Scopable Admin to grant you access.
</Note>

## Where to Manage Integrations

Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** to view, connect, and configure every integration. Each integration tile shows its current status (connected, syncing, error, or not connected) and links to its configuration and client mapping pages.

## Integration Categories

Scopable organizes its integrations into five categories based on the type of data they provide.

### PSA Systems

PSA integrations are the backbone of your client data in Scopable. They sync companies, contacts, open opportunities, configuration items, and your product catalog.

### Microsoft

Microsoft integrations connect Scopable to your clients' Microsoft 365 tenants and your Azure Partner Center (CSP) account, surfacing user counts, MFA posture, license assignments, subscription costs, and AI optimization opportunities.

- **Microsoft 365** — users, MFA status, and license data per client tenant
- **Azure / Partner Center (CSP)** — CSP subscription inventory, cost data, and AI-assisted optimization recommendations

### Distributors

Distributor integrations pull live product catalog data, pricing, and availability directly into the Scopable quoting engine. When you build a quote, Scopable fetches real-time pricing from every connected distributor so your margins are always based on current costs.

- **Pax8** — cloud products and licensing
- **Ingram Micro** — hardware, software, and cloud catalog
- **TD SYNNEX** — full product catalog with live pricing
- **CDW** — hardware and solution pricing

### Other

- **SimpleSAT** — imports customer satisfaction scores and feedback into client records so you can see CSAT trends alongside your QBR and renewal data

## How Integrations Work

Every integration follows the same four-step lifecycle: connect your credentials, let Scopable run an initial pull, confirm client mappings, and then let the regular sync schedule keep everything current.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the integration">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations**, select the integration you want to add, and follow the connection wizard. Each integration walks you through the credentials it needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scopable runs an initial sync">
    After successful authentication, Scopable immediately pulls your organizations, contacts, and catalog data. Initial syncs can take a few minutes depending on the size of your data set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review client mappings">
    Scopable automatically tries to match external organizations to your Scopable clients by name. Review the results at **Settings → Integrations → [Integration] → Client Mappings** and confirm, adjust, or add mappings manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Data flows into your workspace">
    Once mappings are confirmed, synced data appears in client records, the quote wizard, Co-Work AI context, and assessments. Scopable re-syncs on a regular schedule to keep everything current.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Scopable **never writes back** to your source systems unless the integration documentation explicitly states otherwise. All integrations listed here are read-only.
</Info>

## Sync Frequency and Manual Re-sync

Each integration syncs automatically on a recurring schedule. If you need fresh data immediately — for example, before starting a QBR or building a renewal quote — you can trigger a manual re-sync from **Settings → Integrations → [Integration] → Sync Now**.
