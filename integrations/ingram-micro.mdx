The Ingram Micro integration connects your reseller account and pulls real-time product catalog, pricing, availability, quotes, and order history into Scopable. With Ingram Micro connected, your team can search the full distributor catalog, price products at your reseller rates, and keep a complete order history — all without switching between platforms.

## What syncs

| Data | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Product catalog** | Full Ingram Micro catalog available for search inside Quote Wizard |
| **Live pricing** | Your reseller pricing returned at search time |
| **Stock availability** | Real-time inventory levels at the time of quoting |
| **Quotes** | Ability to pull Ingram Micro quotes into Scopable for reference |
| **Order history** | Past orders surfaced within Scopable for reconciliation and reference |

## Prerequisites

Before connecting, confirm you have:

- An **Ingram Micro reseller account** with API access enabled
- Your **API Key** and **Subscription Key** from the Ingram Micro developer portal — see Step 1 below
- **Admin** permissions in Scopable

## Connect Ingram Micro

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an app in the Ingram Micro portal">
    Log in to the **Ingram Micro reseller portal** and navigate to **Developer → My Apps → Create an App**. Complete the app creation form, then copy your **API Key** and **Subscription Key**. Store both values securely before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Ingram Micro integration in Scopable">
    In Scopable, go to **Settings → Integrations → Ingram Micro** and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Paste your **API Key** and **Subscription Key** into the fields provided and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validation and catalog sync">
    Scopable validates your credentials and begins syncing catalog data. Because the Ingram Micro catalog is extensive, the initial index may take several minutes before all products are searchable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After connecting

- **Quote Wizard** — Ingram Micro appears as a selectable catalog source. Product searches return live-priced results at your reseller rates along with current stock levels.
- **Order history** — past Ingram Micro orders are visible within Scopable for billing reconciliation and re-order reference.

<Tip>
  The Ingram Micro catalog contains hundreds of thousands of SKUs. Use specific search terms — part numbers, brand names, or short product descriptions — in Quote Wizard to get the most relevant results quickly.
</Tip>
