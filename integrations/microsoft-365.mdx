The Microsoft 365 integration discovers your managed customer tenants and pulls users, MFA enrollment status, and license assignments directly into Scopable — no app registration required. Once connected, every mapped client gets a dedicated Microsoft 365 tab giving you an at-a-glance view of your customers' identity posture and licensing without switching tools.

## What syncs

| Data | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Managed customer tenants** | Fetched from your delegated admin relationships and matched to Scopable clients |
| **Users** | Display name, UPN, account status, and last sign-in |
| **MFA enrollment status** | Per-user MFA registered / not registered |
| **License assignments** | Product name and assigned seat count per user |

## Prerequisites

Before connecting, confirm you have:

- A Microsoft 365 account with **Global Administrator** access or appropriate delegated admin permissions across your managed customer tenants
- **Admin** permissions in Scopable

## Connect Microsoft 365

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Microsoft 365 integration">
    In Scopable, go to **Settings → Integrations → Microsoft 365** and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Microsoft">
    Click **Sign in with Microsoft**. You are redirected to Microsoft's sign-in and consent screen. Sign in with your admin account and approve the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tenant fetch begins">
    Scopable retrieves your managed customer tenants and starts matching them to your existing Scopable clients. This initial fetch typically completes within a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review client mappings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Microsoft 365 → Client Mappings**. Confirm auto-matched clients and manually link any tenants that weren't matched automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  No app registration is required. Scopable uses a managed, Microsoft-verified application — there are no client secrets or certificates for you to manage or rotate.
</Note>

## After connecting

Once a tenant is mapped to a client, open that client record and select the **Microsoft 365** tab. You'll see:

- A full user list with display name and UPN
- Each user's MFA enrollment status (**Registered** or **Not registered**)
- License assignments with product names and quantities

Use this view during QBRs or security reviews to quickly identify users without MFA enrolled or seats that may be over- or under-provisioned.

## Microsoft 365 vs. Azure / Partner Center

<Note>
  The Microsoft 365 and Azure / Partner Center integrations are separate connections that cover different data sets.

  - **Microsoft 365** — users, MFA status, and license assignments
  - **Azure / Partner Center** — CSP subscriptions, spend data, and AI cost-optimization recommendations

  You can connect both integrations independently. See [Connect Azure Partner Center to Scopable](/integrations/azure-partner-center) for details on the Azure side.
</Note>
