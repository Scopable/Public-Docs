The Azure / Partner Center integration connects your Microsoft Partner Center account and pulls your managed customers' Azure subscriptions, cost data, and resource inventory into Scopable — all via a one-click OAuth flow with no API keys or client secrets to manage. Once connected, each mapped client surfaces a live Azure cost summary alongside AI-generated recommendations to reduce spend, tighten security, and improve reliability.

## What it provides

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Azure subscription list** | All active subscriptions per mapped client |
| **Monthly cost estimates** | Current month's spend with month-over-month change |
| **Cost breakdown** | Spend by Azure service and individual resource |
| **AI cost-optimization recommendations** | Actionable suggestions across cost, security, performance, and reliability tiers |

## Prerequisites

Before connecting, confirm you have:

- **Admin** permissions in Scopable with the **Manage Tenant Settings** permission enabled
- Access to **Microsoft Partner Center** with consent authority for your organization
- **GDAP (Granular Delegated Admin Privileges)** relationships established with the customers you want to pull cost data for — your GDAP roles must include subscription and cost-management read access

## Connect Azure / Partner Center

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Azure / Partner Center integration">
    In Scopable, go to **Settings → Integrations → Azure / Partner Center** and click **Connect Partner Center**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and approve the consent screen">
    Microsoft redirects you to its sign-in and consent screen. Sign in with an admin account that has consent authority in your Partner Center organization and approve the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Scopable">
    Microsoft redirects you back to Scopable automatically. The connection is stored and Scopable immediately begins fetching your managed customer list from Partner Center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review client mappings">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations → Azure / Partner Center → Client Mappings**. Confirm auto-matched customers and manually confirm any suggestions where the match wasn't strong enough to link automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  No API keys, client secrets, or certificates are required. Scopable uses a Microsoft-verified application and a standard OAuth 2.0 authorization code flow — the token is stored securely on your behalf.
</Note>

## Customer matching

Scopable matches Partner Center customers to Scopable clients by company name. Strong name matches link automatically. Weaker matches appear as **suggestions** in the Client Mappings screen for you to confirm or reassign. If a customer's name differs significantly between Partner Center and Scopable, align the names or manually confirm the suggestion.

Customers with no active Azure subscriptions are skipped during the sync. This is expected behavior — not an error.

## Azure / Partner Center vs. Microsoft 365

<Note>
  The Azure / Partner Center and Microsoft 365 integrations are separate connections that cover different data sets.

  - **Azure / Partner Center** — CSP subscriptions, spend data, and AI cost-optimization recommendations
  - **Microsoft 365** — users, MFA enrollment status, and license assignments

  You can connect both independently. See [Connect Microsoft 365 to Scopable](/integrations/microsoft-365) for details on the M365 side.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Sync failed or cost data is missing for a customer">
  Verify the following for that customer:

  1. The customer is **mapped** to a Scopable client in **Settings → Integrations → Azure / Partner Center → Client Mappings**.
  2. The customer has at least one **active Azure subscription** in Partner Center.
  3. Your **GDAP relationship** with that customer grants subscription and cost-management read access. GDAP roles that don't include these scopes will cause cost data to be silently skipped.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The Connect button does nothing">
  The **Connect Partner Center** button requires the **Manage Tenant Settings** permission in Scopable. Ask an Admin to grant you that permission, then retry.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A customer was matched to the wrong Scopable client">
  This happens when company names differ between Partner Center and Scopable. To fix it:

  1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Azure / Partner Center → Client Mappings**.
  2. Find the incorrect match and click **Reassign**.
  3. Select the correct Scopable client from the list and confirm.
</Accordion>
